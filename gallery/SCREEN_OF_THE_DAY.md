# Screen of the Day

A daily ritual. Every morning at 07:00 a new animated screen is created for the
128x64 panel and published to this gallery, together with a **phrase of the day**.
Nikolay awards each one a 👍 or a 👎. The month is tallied at the bottom, and the
best ones are the pride of the shelf.

Honesty rule: the screen shown here is always the real one that ran on the panel —
never a mock-up, never gamed for a thumbs-up. The only way to earn the 👍 is to
make something genuinely good.
